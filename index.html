Finance that reaches everyone.

I'm Professor Syed Shahid Mazhar, Professor and Faculty Affairs Coordinator at Integral Business School, Integral University, Lucknow. Across more than two decades in finance education, my work has centred on one question: how finance can be made to work for the people and businesses it usually overlooks.

My research spans financial inclusion, microfinance, MSMEs, behavioural finance, digital finance, and international business, with a growing line of work on explainable artificial intelligence in finance. I have led a department for more than five years, supervised nineteen doctoral scholars to completion, and helped design an international double-degree programme with Széchenyi István University, Hungary.

My work appears in journals including Artificial Intelligence Review, Financial and Credit Activity: Problems of Theory and Practice, and the Journal of Information and Optimization Sciences. I review for PLOS ONE, IGI Global, and the Journal of Infrastructure, Policy and Development, among others.

01

Research

What makes finance accessible, transparent, and trustworthy for the people it is meant to serve?

Financial inclusion & microfinance

Studying how microfinance institutions, self-help groups, and digital finance extend financial access to rural and underserved populations, with a focus on women's economic empowerment.

MSMEs & entrepreneurship

Examining digital transformation, financing constraints, and the institutional support structures, including SIDBI's role, that shape small and medium enterprise growth in India.

Behavioural & digital finance

Investigating investor behaviour, financial literacy, and consumer adoption of digital payment gateways and fintech services.

Explainable AI in finance

Systematic reviews of model-agnostic explainability methods and their emerging applications in financial decision-making, risk, and ethical AI governance.

Current funded research

Behavioural finance meets explainability

Co-Principal Investigator on “Behavioral Finance Meets Explainability: Exploring Investor Perceptions, Applications and Challenges in Kuwait's Market,” funded by Abdullah Al Salem University, Kuwait (sanctioned amount: KD 30,000).
